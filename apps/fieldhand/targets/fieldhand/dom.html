Human in the loop

Autonomy, with a human hand on the wheel.

Agents handle the routine. When something needs judgment, they pause, explain their reasoning and wait for the right person.

  • Approval gates

    Require a sign-off before an agent moves money, emails a customer or changes a record.

  • Confidence thresholds

    Decisions below the confidence you set go to the right teammate, with the full context attached.

  • A complete audit trail

    Every action, input and decision is logged, searchable and ready for review.
