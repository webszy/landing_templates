Make it yours

A profile photo helps teammates recognize you across shared workspaces and comments.

Add or change your photo
  1. Go to Settings → Profile.
  2. Select your avatar and choose Upload photo.
  3. Crop to fit, then save.

For the sharpest result, use a square image at least 400×400 pixels in JPG or PNG format.
